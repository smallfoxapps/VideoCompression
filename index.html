Compress Video - Reduce Size – Privacy Policy Effective 15 October 2026 · Applies to the Android app “Compress Video - Reduce Size” (com.tsembaiy.videocompression) In short: the app compresses videos on your phone. Your videos are never uploaded, and there is no account, no server of our own, no analytics and no crash reporting. The only things that use the internet are Google’s ads (when you choose to watch one) and Google Play purchases. 1. Your videos The app only opens the videos you choose in Android’s system picker. It does not browse or scan your gallery. Compression happens entirely on your phone, using its built-in video encoder. Compressed copies are saved to the folder you choose on your phone (by default Movies / Compressed Videos). They keep the original’s recording date (and location, if the original has one) so they sort next to the original in your gallery. Your videos, their contents and their file details are never sent anywhere by the app. The app deletes an original only when you ask it to, after Android asks you to confirm. 2. What stays on your phone To work, the app keeps a few things in its private storage on your phone: Settings you choose (theme, default compression mode, save folder and similar). Compression history: file names, sizes, dates and where the copies were saved, so you can find them again. You can delete entries or all of it in the History screen. Size learning: a few numbers about how your phone’s encoder hits target sizes, so later videos fit better. Temporary files (compressed videos before saving, previews, thumbnails), cleaned up automatically. Whether you bought “Remove ads”, and which day your free export was used. None of this is uploaded. It is excluded from Android backup and device-to-device transfer, and it is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. 3. Ads (Google AdMob) The app is free. Your first compression each day needs no ad; after that, the app may offer a short rewarded video ad from Google AdMob before compressing, which you can skip by buying “Remove ads”. To show ads, Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as your device’s advertising ID, your IP address (which gives an approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This is used for showing ads, measuring them and preventing fraud. This information is collected by Google, not by us, and handled under Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Where the law requires consent (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for it with Google’s consent form first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. The app turns off Android’s Privacy Sandbox ad topics and ad attribution. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings (Settings → Google → Ads on most phones). After you buy “Remove ads”, the app stops loading and showing ads. 4. Purchases (Google Play) “Remove ads” is a one-time purchase made through Google Play Billing. Payment is handled entirely by Google; the app never sees your payment details. The app only receives whether the purchase is complete, so it can remove ads and restore the purchase on a new phone with the same Google account. 5.
